    About & disclaimer

    Not for navigation. An informational overlay of where you can run, built from volunteer-contributed OpenStreetMap data. Sidewalks, gates and access rules are incompletely mapped — a missing line does not always mean "no running", and a present line does not guarantee safe or legal passage. Verify on the ground.

    Data updates: the OSM snapshot is rebuilt monthly. The date below is the snapshot this page is using.

    Coverage: the dashed outline shows the exact area of the source extract (Geofabrik “europe”). Outside it the map has no data.
